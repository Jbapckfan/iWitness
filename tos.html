2. Purpose of the Service

OnTheRecord is designed to help individuals document situations where they feel unsafe, threatened, or at risk. The App is intended for:

  • Recording interactions with law enforcement
  • Documenting potential workplace harassment or discrimination
  • Personal safety while traveling alone (walking to your car, public transit, etc.)
  • Domestic violence documentation (from the victim's perspective)
  • Any situation where you feel your safety may be at risk

OnTheRecord is designed to protect the recorder, not enable perpetrators. The App is a tool for documenting evidence of potential wrongdoing against you, not for recording illegal activities you are committing.

4. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

OnTheRecord is built with a zero-knowledge encryption architecture. This means:

  • We cannot access your videos. All encryption keys are generated and stored exclusively on your device.
  • We cannot decrypt your content. Even if subpoenaed, we are technically unable to provide decrypted video content because we do not possess the keys.
  • We cannot recover your data. If you lose your device and have not set up Trusted Contacts, your encrypted data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Your privacy is protected by mathematics, not policy. Our zero-knowledge architecture ensures that only you (and your designated Trusted Contacts) can ever access your recordings.

5. Data Ownership

  • You own your data. All videos, metadata, and associated content belong exclusively to you.
  • Only you can edit or delete your files. Trusted Contacts have view-only access and cannot modify or remove your content.
  • We store only encrypted blobs. On our servers (if you use cloud backup), we store only encrypted data that we cannot read.

6. Trusted Contacts

The Trusted Contact feature allows you to designate individuals who can access your recordings under specific conditions:

  • View-Only Access: Trusted Contacts can view and download but cannot edit or delete your videos
  • You control access: You choose which videos each contact can see
  • Revocable at any time: You can remove a Trusted Contact's access instantly
  • Emergency access: If you become incapacitated, Trusted Contacts may access videos based on the conditions you set (e.g., no check-in for 48 hours)

7. Emergency and Incapacitation

If you are detained, injured, or killed, the videos you have shared with Trusted Contacts remain accessible to them. This ensures that evidence survives even if you cannot personally retrieve or share it.

We recommend setting up at least one Trusted Contact (such as a lawyer, family member, or close friend) to ensure your evidence can be accessed if something happens to you.
