Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

1. Introduction

OnTheRecord is built on a zero-knowledge architecture. We believe your evidence belongs to you and only you. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, what we don't collect, and why our technical design makes your privacy a mathematical certainty rather than a policy promise.

Our core principle: We cannot access your recordings, your encryption keys, or your personal data. This is enforced by our architecture, not just our policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the absolute minimum required to operate the service:

  • Device information for crash reporting: Anonymous crash logs including device model, iOS version, and app version to diagnose and fix bugs.
  • Email address (optional): Only if you create an account for cloud backup features. Used solely for account authentication and critical service communications.

What We Do NOT Collect

OnTheRecord is specifically designed to avoid collecting sensitive information:

  • Video or audio content — All recordings remain on your device or are encrypted client-side before upload. We never have access to plaintext footage.
  • Location data — GPS coordinates are embedded in your recordings locally but are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Encryption keys — Keys are generated and stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on your device. We never possess, transmit, or escrow your keys.
  • Contacts or address book — Emergency contact information is stored locally on your device only.
  • Analytics or behavioral data — We do not use any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking pixels.
  • Biometric data — Face ID / Touch ID authentication is handled entirely by iOS and never leaves the Secure Enclave.

3. Data Storage & Encryption

OnTheRecord uses strong, industry-standard encryption to protect your recordings:

  • AES-256-GCM client-side encryption for all video chunks before they leave your device.
  • RSA-4096 key wrapping to protect per-chunk encryption keys.
  • P256 ECDSA digital signatures to verify evidence integrity and chain of custody.
  • iOS Keychain with Secure Enclave protection for all cryptographic keys.
  • User-configured destinations — You choose where your encrypted data is stored: your own NAS, WebDAV server, S3-compatible storage, or Cloudflare R2. We do not operate storage servers.

Even if our servers were seized, the data stored there is encrypted with keys we do not possess. Decryption is mathematically impossible without your device.

4. Third-Party Services

OnTheRecord integrates with the following third-party services, each with strict data minimization:

  • Twilio (SMS routing only): Used to send emergency SMS alerts to your designated contacts. Twilio processes only the phone numbers you configure and the alert message content. No video or audio data is transmitted through Twilio.
  • Your own cloud storage: If you configure offsite backup, encrypted data is uploaded to your own storage provider (NAS, S3, R2, WebDAV). We have no access to these credentials or stored data.

We do not use: Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Firebase, Amplitude, Mixpanel, advertising networks, or any other analytics, tracking, or advertising services. Zero telemetry. Zero tracking. Zero compromise.

5. Data Retention

You have complete control over your data:

  • Local recordings: Stored on your device until you delete them. The app does not auto-delete recordings.
  • Offsite backups: Stored in your configured storage destination until you delete them. We cannot delete data from your storage because we have no access to it.
  • Account data: If you have an account, you can request deletion at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Crash logs: Anonymized crash reports are retained for 90 days, then automatically purged.

6. Law Enforcement Requests

OnTheRecord's zero-knowledge architecture means we are technically unable to comply with decryption requests:

  • We do not hold encryption keys for any user's data.
  • We cannot decrypt, access, or produce plaintext recordings.
  • We cannot identify which encrypted data blobs belong to which user.
  • If served with a valid legal order, we can only provide the minimal account information we hold (email address, if an account exists).

This is by design. We built OnTheRecord so that compliance with overreaching data requests is technically impossible. Your privacy is protected by mathematics, not policy.

7. Children's Privacy

OnTheRecord is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the App or via email (if you have an account). The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

Continued use of OnTheRecord after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
