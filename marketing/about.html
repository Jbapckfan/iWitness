OnTheRecord is an open-source iOS application that ensures video evidence of critical encounters survives — even if the device is seized, destroyed, or confiscated.

Overview

What is OnTheRecord?

OnTheRecord is an iOS app that records, encrypts, and offloads video evidence in real time. Built for journalists, activists, domestic violence survivors, and anyone who needs their recordings to survive hostile conditions.

Unlike standard camera apps, OnTheRecord writes encrypted 2-second chunks directly to disk and uploads them to user-controlled offsite storage as they're captured. If the phone dies, is seized, or is destroyed — the footage has already left the device.

2 sec

Maximum data loss window. Chunks are written and uploaded every 2 seconds.

256-bit

AES-GCM encryption with client-side keys that never leave the device.

AGPLv3

Fully open source. Auditable. Cannot be silently shut down.

Capabilities

Key Features

Dual-Camera Recording

Simultaneous front and rear camera capture creates an immutable timeline showing both the subject and the recorder's perspective.

Zero-Knowledge Encryption

AES-256-GCM client-side encryption. Keys never leave the device. Even under subpoena, decryption is mathematically impossible without the user's device.

Real-Time Offsite Upload

Encrypted chunks upload to user-controlled storage (NAS, S3, R2, WebDAV) as they're captured. Evidence leaves the device within seconds.

Calculator Camouflage

App disguises itself as a functional calculator. Hidden interface prevents detection by aggressors who might search the phone.

Emergency Alert Network

Automatic SMS alerts with GPS coordinates sent to designated contacts when recording begins. Includes Apple Watch companion for wrist activation.

SHA-256 Evidence Chain

Each chunk is cryptographically hashed at capture. Any tampering breaks the chain, providing court-grade proof of authenticity.
