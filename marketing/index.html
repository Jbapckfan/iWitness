Ariel et al., J. Quant. Criminology, 2015

How It Works

From voice to vault in seconds

01

“Hey Siri, put this on the record”

Or tap once. Or press your Apple Watch. Recording starts instantly, even from your lock screen.

02

Your camera. Your perspective.

Your rear camera records continuously. Pro users get full front-facing video too — so if someone grabs your phone and looks at the screen, they don't realize the front camera is recording their face.

03

Encrypted and uploaded in real time

Every half second, an encrypted chunk is sent to your cloud. Unlike iCloud Photos — which can be stopped by airplane mode and deleted — our chunks are already gone before anyone can intervene.

04

Share on your terms

Export a complete evidence package, share with your lawyer, send to someone you trust, or keep it locked. You decide.
