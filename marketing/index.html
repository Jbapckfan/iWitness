Built Different

Unlike standard camera apps, OnTheRecord was engineered from the ground up for one purpose: ensuring your evidence survives.

01

Zero-RAM Architecture

Video chunks are written directly to disk, never held in memory. If iOS terminates the app or your battery dies, no data is lost. Most camera apps buffer in RAM and lose everything on crash.

02

AES-GCM Encryption

Every video chunk is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. Keys are stored in the Secure Enclave via Keychain. Even if someone extracts your device storage, the footage is unreadable.

03

SHA-256 Evidence Chain

Each chunk is hashed at capture time. Any tampering—even a single frame—changes the hash, proving the evidence is unaltered. Courts increasingly require this level of verification.

04

Background Resilience

Uploads continue even when the app is backgrounded or the screen is locked. Uses iOS background URLSession to survive app suspension—your evidence reaches the cloud no matter what.
