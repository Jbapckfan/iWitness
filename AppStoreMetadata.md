## Description (4000 characters max)

OnTheRecord turns your iPhone into a tamper-proof personal safety recorder with real-time encrypted backup -- so your footage is safe even if your phone isn't.

Whether you're documenting a traffic stop, recording a landlord interaction, capturing workplace misconduct, or simply wanting peace of mind during a late-night walk, OnTheRecord ensures your recordings are encrypted, backed up, and verifiable from the moment you press record.

KEY FEATURES

- One-Tap Activation -- Start recording instantly with a single tap, your Apple Watch, or just say "Hey Siri, put this on the record."

- Dual-Camera Recording -- Capture what's in front of you and what's facing you simultaneously, giving the full picture of any encounter.

- Real-Time Encrypted Backup -- Every half-second, your recording is encrypted and uploaded to your personal cloud or NAS. Even if your phone is taken, your footage is already safe.

- Military-Grade Encryption -- AES-256-GCM encryption with RSA-4096 key wrapping protects your recordings. Only you hold the keys.

- Tamper-Proof Chain of Custody -- Every recording chunk is digitally signed and hash-chained with timestamps, creating a verifiable record that proves your footage hasn't been altered.

- Live Transcription -- Real-time speech-to-text overlay on your recordings, so you have both audio and a written record.

- Emergency Alerts -- Automatically notify trusted contacts via SMS or email when you start recording, so someone always knows.

- Apple Watch Companion -- Start, stop, and monitor recordings from your wrist without ever reaching for your phone.

- Stealth Mode -- A blackout display option lets you record discreetly when showing a recording screen could escalate a situation.

- Geofence Auto-Recording -- Set locations where recording starts automatically, so you never forget to press record when it matters most.

- Witness Mesh -- Nearby OnTheRecord users can receive encrypted backup copies of your recording in real time, creating a peer-to-peer safety network.

- Evidence Export -- Generate a court-ready evidence package with a chain-of-custody PDF, digital signature verification, and hash integrity proof.

- Recovery Kit -- A secure backup system for your encryption keys, so you never lose access to your recordings.

- App Lock -- Biometric authentication prevents anyone else from accessing your recordings or settings.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Set up in minutes. Create your encryption keys, choose your backup destination (iCloud, NAS, or other cloud storage), and add your trusted contacts.

2. Record when it matters. Tap the record button, use your Apple Watch, or say "Hey Siri, put this on the record." Your phone immediately starts capturing, encrypting, and uploading.

3. Your footage is safe. Encrypted chunks are backed up in real time. Export evidence packages whenever you need them, with full chain-of-custody documentation.

YOUR PRIVACY IS THE POINT

OnTheRecord is open source. You can inspect every line of code. Your encryption keys are generated on your device and never leave it. We cannot access your recordings -- ever. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your safety data belongs to you alone.
